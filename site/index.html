What it does

Slower, same pitch
Anywhere from 0.25× to 3×, with presets one click away. Or the other way round: shift the pitch by semitones and cents and keep the tempo.
Loop the hard bit
Shift-drag across the waveform and it loops, gaplessly. Drag the edges until it's exactly the bar.
Marks
Tap M along with the music to drop points, R twice for a region. Name them, colour them, jump between them.
Video, in sync
Lessons and live clips play frame-exact at any speed. Put the picture in its own window on a second screen, or make it big.
Straight from YouTube
Paste a link, or just type what you're looking for. Album chapters become regions. Only download what you have the rights to.
Projects
Save your marks, loop, speed and EQ in a .sbee file, linked to the song or with the song inside.
Long files
An hour-long concert opens in seconds. Two hours works too.
Offline
Everything runs on your machine. Only downloading needs the internet.

Hands stay on the keys

Transcribing is listen, stop, back up, listen again, so every step of it has a key. Press ? in the app for the full list.

  • SpacePlay, or replay from where you started listening
  • ←→Step back and forward
  • ↑↓Change the step, 0.01 s to 10 s
  • []LSet the loop and turn it on
  • MMark a point where you hear it
  • RStart a region, and end it
  • SStart from here next time
  • VFVideo in its own window, or big
