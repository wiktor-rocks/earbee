It hears the beat.
You loop the bar.

EarBee finds the beats and bars for you, so you can loop one bar at a time.

Find the beats and bars

Press B. A line marks every beat, a stronger line the start of every bar, and you see the tempo.

  • ← → jump to the next beat or bar.
  • Shift + B plays a click on every beat.

On Intel Macs: beats only, no bars.

Loop a bar, then the next

Press \ to loop the bar you're in.

  • = doubles the loop, - halves it.
  • Shift + ] moves it to the next phrase, Shift + [ back.
  • Loop edges snap to the beats. Hold Alt to place them freely.

What it does

Slower, same pitch
Play from 0.25× to 3× speed without changing the pitch. Or change the pitch, not the speed.
Beats and bars
Press B to mark every beat and bar, and see the tempo.
Loops
Shift-drag across the waveform, or press \ to loop the current bar. It repeats without a gap.
Marks
Press M to mark a point, R twice to mark a section. Name and colour them.
Video
Videos stay in sync at any speed. Show them big, or in a window on a second screen.
From YouTube
Paste a link, or search by name. Chapters become marked sections.
Projects
Save your marks, loop, speed and EQ in one .ebee file, with or without the song.
Long files, offline
An hour-long concert opens in seconds. No internet needed, except to download.

Hands stay on the keys

Listen, stop, go back, listen again: each step is one key. Press ? in the app for the full list.

  • SpacePlay, or replay from where you started
  • ←→Step back and forward
  • ↑↓Step size: 0.01 s to 10 s, a beat or a bar
  • BFind the beats
  • []LLoop start, loop end, loop on or off
  • \Loop the current bar
  • =-Double or halve the loop
  • Shift[]Move the loop back or on
  • MMark a point
  • RMark a section: start, then end
  • SStart from here next time
  • VFVideo in a window, or big
