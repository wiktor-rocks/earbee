## What EarBee does about it

EarBee is a desktop player made for this one job:

- **Slower, same pitch.** Play from 0.25× to 3× speed without changing the pitch.
- **Beats and bars.** Press <kbd>B</kbd> and it marks every beat and bar, so you can step through the song one beat at a time.
- **Loop the hard bar.** Press <kbd>\\</kbd> to loop the bar you're in, then move the loop on, one phrase at a time.
- **Hands on the keys.** Listen, stop, go back, listen again: each step is one key.

It's free, with no account and no ads, and the code is [on GitHub](https://github.com/wiktor-rocks/earbee).
